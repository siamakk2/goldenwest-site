Questions

Wholesale, answered

Does Golden West sell to the public or only wholesale?

Golden West is a wholesale supplier serving car washes, express tunnels, auto detailers, and fleet operators across Southern California. Request pricing and we'll set you up with a wholesale account.

Do you manufacture your own car wash chemicals?

Yes. We've formulated and manufactured our own line of car wash and industrial cleaning products in Gardena, California since 1990, and we also stock trusted brands like 3M, P&S, Malco and Stoner.

Do you deliver, and how much does it cost?

We deliver free within a 50-mile radius of Gardena on orders of $500 or more. For locations beyond that radius, we arrange shipping.

What sizes do your products come in?

Most chemicals are available from 5-gallon pails up to 55-gallon drums, with 275-gallon totes for high-volume tunnels. Supplies and equipment are sold by the unit or by the case.

Can I get Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for your chemicals?

Yes. Current SDS sheets are available on request for every chemical product we supply.

Do you offer service in Spanish?

Yes. Our team assists customers in English and Spanish. Call (310) 213-3171 for service in Spanish.

How do I get wholesale pricing?

Add the products you're interested in to your quote, then submit the request form with your business details and shipping ZIP. We'll send wholesale pricing and delivery options quickly.
