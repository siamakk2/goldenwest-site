Car wash soap in bulk — what we make

Our soap line covers every stage of a wash: hyper-concentrated low-pH soaps, high-foam presoaks, wash-and-wax combinations, triple-foam Rainbow Wax in blue, red, yellow and green, waterless wash soap for rinse-limited sites, and degreasers for wheels, engines and mats. Everything ships in 1-gallon jugs, 5-gallon pails, and 30 or 55-gallon drums, so a single-bay hand wash and a high-volume express tunnel buy the same chemistry at the container size that fits their throughput.

Why manufacturer-direct beats "cheap" wholesale

Searching for cheap wholesale car wash soap usually surfaces resellers moving diluted product. Concentration is what actually decides your cost per car: a hyper-concentrate that dilutes 1:128 costs less per vehicle than a bargain drum cut to 1:20 before it reaches you. Because we control the formula, we will tell you the dilution ratio and help you dial in your proportioners — that is where the savings are, not on the sticker.
