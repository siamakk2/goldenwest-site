Common questions

Where is the Golden West car wash supply store?

At our factory: 1538 West 132nd Street, Gardena, CA 90249 — minutes off the 110 and 91 freeways, in the South Bay area of Los Angeles County.

What are the store hours?

Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call (310) 538-0918 — or (310) 213-3171 for service in Spanish — to confirm availability before you drive over.

Can the public buy, or is it wholesale only?

Anyone can buy at published retail prices — walk in, or order on the shop page. Car washes, detailers and fleets can open a wholesale account for trade pricing.

How does will-call pickup work?

Call ahead with what you need and we'll have it ready when you arrive. Soaps and presoaks are made on site, so bulk sizes from 5-gallon pails to 55-gallon drums are picked up at the source.

Do you also deliver?

Yes — across Los Angeles, the South Bay, Long Beach and Orange County with freight by ZIP code, and drum orders ship elsewhere in the USA.
