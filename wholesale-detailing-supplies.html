Common questions

Can I pick up detailing supplies instead of paying freight?

Yes — will-call pickup at our Gardena warehouse, 1538 West 132nd Street, Monday to Friday 8 AM to 5 PM. Many local detailers pick up weekly.

Do you supply mobile detailers?

Yes. Mobile detailers typically buy 5-gallon pails of soap and degreaser plus towels and applicators by the case. No large minimums.

Is everything your own brand?

Chemistry is formulated and made by Golden West in Gardena; we also carry select professional lines such as 3M, P&S, Malco, Stoner and Simoniz.

Do you deliver detailing supplies near me?

If you are in Los Angeles, the South Bay, Long Beach or Orange County, yes — freight is calculated by your ZIP code at checkout, and drum orders elsewhere in the USA ship by arrangement.
