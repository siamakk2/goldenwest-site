The tunnel sequence, from our tanks

We make the core tunnel chemistry in-house: heavy-duty presoak that opens road film before friction or high pressure, hyper-concentrated wash soaps, triple-color foam conditioner in blue, red, yellow, and green for the show your customers pay for, and sheeting drying agents that pull water off the panel before the blowers. Common questions

What chemicals does an express tunnel need?

The core sequence is presoak to break road film, wash soap, triple-foam conditioner, and a drying agent before the blowers. Golden West manufactures all four in Gardena, California, plus tire, trim, and glass finishing products.

Do you make chemicals for touchless car washes?

Yes — our concentrated, biodegradable Touchless soap is formulated to deliver cleaning power without friction, for touchless tunnels and in-bay automatics.

What is triple foam?

Triple-color foam conditioner applied mid-tunnel in blue, red, yellow, or green — it conditions the paint and gives customers a visible premium step. Golden West manufactures it in all four colors.

What sizes do tunnel chemicals come in?

30 and 55-gallon drums and 275-gallon totes for tunnels; 5-gallon pails are available for lower-volume washes.
