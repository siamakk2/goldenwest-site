Common questions

What is Black Magic car wax?

Black Magic Wax is Golden West's deep-gloss house wax, formulated for a wet-look shine on dark paint. Detailers apply it as the finishing step after compounding. It's available at retail on the shop page and at wholesale case pricing for shops.

Carnauba or synthetic — which wax should a detail shop stock?

Both. Carnauba paste and creme give the warm, deep shine customers ask for on dark and show-car finishes; synthetic blends like Black Magic Wax are faster to work and last longer. Most shops stock one of each.

Which buffing compound grade do I need?

Heavy-duty compounds remove sanding marks and heavy oxidation, medium compounds clear swirls and light scratches, and finishing waxes restore gloss. If you're unsure, call (310) 538-0918 — the chemist who makes them will tell you.
